Simple Grades, Clear Meaning

No technical jargon — just a letter that tells you how well a model will work on your machine

S

Excellent

Runs fast, uses reasonable memory. Best choice for your hardware.

A

Great

Good speed and memory. A solid choice for most tasks.

B

Good

Works well but may be slightly slower or use more memory.

C

Adequate

Fits but tight. Consider a smaller version of this model.

D

Marginal

Very tight fit. May be slow or unstable on your hardware.

F

Too Heavy

Won't fit on your hardware. You need more RAM or a better GPU.
